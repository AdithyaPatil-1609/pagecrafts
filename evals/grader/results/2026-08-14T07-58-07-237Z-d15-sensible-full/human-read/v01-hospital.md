## Plan
- hero/split-image — Show a striking image of the hospital with a tagline about compassionate, advanced care for Nashik’s community.
- about/media-split — Summarize the hospital’s 40‑bed history, mission, values, and overview of its modern facilities in Nashik.
- services/cards — List the three core specialties – Cardiology, Orthopedics, General Medicine – with brief treatment options for each.
- team/grid — job titles this practice needs, one role per row — never "Attorney Name" or a dummy name; years of practice only if the description gives a number
- testimonials/quotes — Feature two or three patient success stories that illustrate effective treatment in the hospital’s specialties.
- faq/accordion — Answer common queries on admissions, insurance coverage, visiting hours, emergency care and billing.
- contact/split-map — Display the emergency phone number prominently, provide phone, email, address, a map of the Nashik location, and a simple appointment request form.

About Our Hospital

Our 40‑bed multi‑speciality hospital in Nashik provides comprehensive care across cardiology, orthopaedics and general medicine. Since opening, we have been dedicated to delivering compassionate, patient‑focused services guided by our core values of excellence, integrity and respect. Our mission is to improve the health of the community through advanced, evidence‑based treatments in a modern, well‑equipped facility. The hospital features state‑of‑the‑art operation theatres, a fully staffed emergency department and comfortable inpatient wards designed for optimal recovery.
