## Plan
- hero/split-image — A warm headline inviting parents to enroll their kids, with a bright “Join Our After‑School Program” button — primary CTA is Donate or Volunteer, not Enroll
- about/media-split — Explain the NGO’s mission to provide safe, fun after‑school classes and how the program benefits the community.
- services/cards — List the activities offered—art, sports, STEM workshops, and homework help—highlighting age groups and schedules.
- gallery/masonry — Show candid photos of children engaging in the various classes and enjoying the program.
- faq/accordion — Answer key questions about program hours, safety measures, volunteer requirements, and donation options.
- contact/form — Provide a simple form for parents to enroll, and for volunteers or donors to sign up, plus email address.
- footer/columns — Include links to About, Activities, Volunteer, Donate, social media icons, and copyright notice.
