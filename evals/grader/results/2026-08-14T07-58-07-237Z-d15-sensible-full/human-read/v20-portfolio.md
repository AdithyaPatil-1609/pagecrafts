## Plan
- hero/minimal — Show a striking headline like 'Freelance Graphic Designer' with a minimal visual and a button to view portfolio.
- about/text — first person about the person who asked — training, why they started, how they teach. Not "our studio"
- services/cards — List core services such as logo design, branding, print layouts, and digital graphics.
- gallery/grid — Display a curated grid of standout project thumbnails linking to larger images.
- contact/simple — Provide a simple contact button linking to a form and icons for social media profiles.
- footer/columns — Show copyright notice, minimal navigation links, and a brief note about availability.
