## Plan
- hero/minimal — Prominently display the blog title “book-blog” with a short tagline about books and slow living, inviting readers to explore.
- about/text — Explain that this is a personal minimal blog focused on books and slow‑living reflections, sharing the author’s purpose and voice.
- services/cards — recent posts about the topics in the description, with real titles and one-line descriptions — never "Add a post title here"
- gallery/grid — Show a curated selection of book‑cover images and cozy reading‑scene photos that reflect the blog’s aesthetic.
- contact/form — Provide a simple contact form for messages and include icons linking to the blog’s social‑media profiles.
- footer/simple — Include minimal legal footer with copyright notice, a link to the privacy policy, and simple navigation links (home, about, posts).
