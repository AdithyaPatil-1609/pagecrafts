## Plan
- hero/minimal — first person: what this person does and where they have worked — never a resume-writing shop, never "Your Name", never packages for clients
- about/text — first person, their own work history in a few sentences. Not a mission to help clients. Do not invent employers or years
- services/timeline — roles and places they have worked — a timeline of their jobs, not resume packages, not prices, not a service sold to clients
- contact/simple — how to reach this person; leave phone, email, address and hours empty unless the description gives them
- footer/columns — one line about this person, not a business selling to clients
