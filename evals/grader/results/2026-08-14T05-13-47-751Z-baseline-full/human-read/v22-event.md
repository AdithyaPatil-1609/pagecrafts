Frequently Asked Questions

3. **question:** Where is the venue located and how can I reach it? · **answer:** The event is at the Hyderabad International Convention Centre, Gachibowli. It is accessible by metro (Blue Line, Gachibowli Station) and by taxi or rideshare.
