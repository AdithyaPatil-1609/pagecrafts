## about · text

**heading**

Our Story

**body**

At the heart of Jayanagar sits our small studio, a place where every student—young or seasoned—finds a welcoming space to learn guitar or keyboard. Founded on the belief that music belongs to all ages, we run weekend batches that fit busy families and working adults alike. Our instructors, each a performing musician, blend patient guidance with real‑world experience, ensuring each lesson feels personal and inspiring. From first chords to polished pieces, our mission is simple: nurture a lifelong love of music in every student.
