## about · text

**heading**

Our Story

**body**

Founded in a modest workshop, our artisan turned a love for pure, natural ingredients into a line of handcrafted soaps and candles. Each bar and each candle is poured by hand, using sustainably sourced oils, butters, and botanical extracts. From the first scent blend to the final packaging, the process stays entirely manual, preserving the care that goes into every product. Though we began as a local shop, we now ship our fragrant creations across India, inviting every guest to experience the warmth of handmade quality.
