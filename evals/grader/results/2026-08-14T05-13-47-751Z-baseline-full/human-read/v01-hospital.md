About Our Hospital

Founded in 2015, our 40‑bed multi‑speciality hospital in Nashik delivers comprehensive care across Cardiology, Orthopaedics and General Medicine. Our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence‑based treatment that improves the health and wellbeing of every patient. Guided by the core values of integrity, excellence, teamwork and patient‑centered service, we combine modern technology with skilled physicians to ensure safe, timely emergency care (call 1800‑123‑456) and ongoing specialist support.

## faq · accordion

**heading**

Frequently Asked Questions

**items**

1. **question:** How can I book an appointment with a specialist? · **answer:** Call our appointment desk at the number provided on the website or use the online booking form to select a department and preferred time.
2. **question:** What are the visiting hours for in‑patients? · **answer:** Visiting hours are from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily; please register at the reception before entering.
3. **question:** Which specialties are available at the hospital? · **answer:** We provide cardiology, orthopedics, and general medicine services within our 40‑bed facility.
4. **question:** How do I reach the 24‑hour emergency service? · **answer:** Dial the emergency number displayed on our website or on the hospital signage; the line is staffed around the clock.
5. **question:** What documents should I bring for my first visit? · **answer:** Please bring a valid photo ID, any referral letters, previous medical records, and your insurance card, if applicable.
