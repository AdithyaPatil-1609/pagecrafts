## Plan
- hero/minimal — Showcase a bold tagline and a clear call-to-action button inviting clients to hire the designer.
- about/text — Brief background highlighting the designer’s experience, style, and story.
- services/cards — List core design services such as branding, web graphics, illustration, and print design.
- gallery/grid — Display a selection of polished portfolio pieces in a clean grid.
- testimonials/quotes — Feature concise client quotes praising the designer’s quality and reliability.
- contact/simple — Provide a short inquiry form and essential contact details with a submit button.
- footer/columns — Include copyright notice, privacy link, and icons linking to social media profiles.
