## about · text

**heading**

About the Residents Association

**body**

Formed in 2015, the Bangalore Residents Association represents the 180 flats of our apartment complex. Our mission is to provide a safe, well‑maintained, and collaborative living environment for every resident. The Association issues official notices, coordinates maintenance, and serves as the liaison between residents and building management.

1. **question:** What are the monthly maintenance fees and what do they cover? · **answer:** Each flat pays a fixed monthly maintenance fee that covers common area cleaning, security, water supply, electricity for lighting in corridors, waste management, and routine repairs of shared facilities.
2. **question:** How can I obtain a copy of the association's rules and regulations? · **answer:** The latest Rules and Regulations are available on the residents portal and can also be requested in hard copy from the association secretary during office hours.
3. **question:** Who are the current committee members and how can I contact them? · **answer:** The committee consists of the President, Vice‑President, Secretary, Treasurer, and four ward members. 4. **question:** How do I report a maintenance issue or request a service? · **answer:** Residents can log a request through the online maintenance portal or call the dedicated helpline (080‑XXXX‑XXXX). A ticket will be generated and assigned to the maintenance team within 24 hours.
