## faq · accordion

**heading**

Frequently Asked Questions

**items**

1. **question:** What factors determine shipping rates? · **answer:** Rates are based on load size, distance, freight type, fuel surcharge, and any required special handling.
2. **question:** How long does delivery take for full‑truck‑load shipments? · **answer:** Typical transit time is 2‑5 business days within the same state and 5‑10 business days for inter‑state deliveries, subject to road conditions and customs clearance where applicable.
3. **question:** Which regions do you serve? · **answer:** We cover Surat and the surrounding Gujarat districts, all of India’s major industrial corridors, and offer cross‑border services to neighboring countries upon request.
4. **question:** How can a client book a shipment? · **answer:** Clients can book online via our portal, call our dispatch desk, or email the operations team; a booking reference is issued within one business hour.
5. **question:** Do you handle partial loads? · **answer:** Yes. Partial loads are consolidated with compatible shipments to optimize space and cost, following the same booking process as full‑truck loads.
