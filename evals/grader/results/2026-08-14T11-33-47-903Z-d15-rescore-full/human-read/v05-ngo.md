## faq · accordion

**heading**

Frequently Asked Questions

**items**

1. **question:** How do I enroll my child in the after‑school program? · **answer:** Simply fill out our online enrollment form or pick up a paper form at our center, submit it with a brief health questionnaire, and we’ll confirm your spot within two business days.
2. **question:** What safety measures are in place for the students? · **answer:** All staff are background‑checked, classrooms are supervised at all times, and we follow strict health and safety protocols including regular sanitising and secure drop‑off/pick‑up procedures.
3. **question:** Are there any costs or fees for the program? · **answer:** Enrollment is free of charge; we rely on donations to cover supplies and activities, so any contribution you can make is appreciated.
4. **question:** Do I need to book a spot in advance or can I drop in? · **answer:** Please book in advance through the enrollment form so we can maintain appropriate student‑to‑staff ratios; walk‑ins are welcome when space allows.
5. **question:** What should my child bring to the first session? · **answer:** A completed health form, a water bottle, a snack if needed, and any required school supplies listed on the enrollment packet.
6. **question:** How can I volunteer or donate? · **answer:** Volunteers can sign up on our website and we’ll match you with a suitable role; donations can be made online or in person and are used directly for classroom materials, field trips, and staff training.
