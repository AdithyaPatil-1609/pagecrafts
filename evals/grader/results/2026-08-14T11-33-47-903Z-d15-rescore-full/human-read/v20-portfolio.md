## Plan
- hero/split-image — Show a striking visual of my design work with a tagline like 'Freelance Graphic Designer – Crafting Visual Solutions'.
- about/media-split — first person about the person who asked — training, why they started, how they teach. Not "our studio"
- services/cards — List offered services: brand identity design, marketing collateral, web graphics, illustration, and social media assets.
- gallery/masonry — Display a selection of past projects showcasing logos, posters, website mockups, and illustrations.
- contact/form — Provide a simple contact form for inquiries and include links to my LinkedIn and email — leave phone, email, address and hours empty unless the description gives them
- footer/simple — Include copyright notice and quick links to portfolio, services, and contact sections.
