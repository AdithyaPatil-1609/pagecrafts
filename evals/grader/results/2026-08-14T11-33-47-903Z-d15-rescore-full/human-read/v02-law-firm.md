## Plan
- hero/split-image — Prominent headline stating the firm's expertise in property and family law, a concise tagline, and a CTA to schedule a consultation.
- about/media-split — Brief history highlighting the firm's years of practice, mission to provide trusted legal counsel, and core values of integrity and client focus.
- services/cards — List and describe the two main practice areas: property law (transactions, disputes) and family law (divorce, custody, support).
- team/grid — job titles this practice needs, one role per row — never "Attorney Name" or a dummy name; years of practice only if the description gives a number
- contact/form — Phone number, email address, office location, and a simple contact form for consultation requests — leave phone, email, address and hours empty unless the description gives them
- footer/columns — Legal disclaimer, quick links to privacy policy, terms, and social media icons.
