## Plan
- hero/split-image — Prominently display the hospital name, 40‑bed multi‑specialty identity in Nashik with a call‑to‑action button for appointments or emergency contact.
- about/media-split — Summarize the hospital’s founding, mission to provide comprehensive care, and core values of compassion, excellence, and community service.
- services/cards — List the main specialties—Cardiology, Orthopedics, General Medicine—and brief descriptions of key patient care services.
- team/grid — job titles this practice needs, one role per row — never "Attorney Name" or a dummy name; years of practice only if the description gives a number
- faq/accordion — Answer common patient questions about admission, visiting hours, insurance, appointment scheduling, and emergency procedures.
- contact/split-map — Show the hospital’s address in Nashik, a map, phone numbers including the emergency line, and a simple inquiry form — leave phone, email, address and hours empty unless the description gives them
- footer/columns — Provide legal disclaimer, accreditation logos, quick links to departments, privacy policy, and contact email.
