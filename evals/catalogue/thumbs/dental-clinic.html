Frequently Asked Questions

What dental services does your clinic provide?

We offer routine check‑ups, cleanings, fillings, root canals, and orthodontic treatments such as braces.

How do I book an appointment?

You can schedule a visit online through our booking portal or call our front desk during business hours.

What are your clinic hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 9 am – 7 pm. Sunday is closed.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we work with most major dental insurance plans and can help you file claims.

What should I expect during a root canal procedure?

The tooth will be numbed, the infected pulp removed, and the canal cleaned and sealed. Most patients report minimal discomfort and can resume normal activities the same day.

How long does orthodontic treatment with braces take?

Treatment duration varies, but most patients wear braces for 12 – 24 months, followed by a retention phase.
