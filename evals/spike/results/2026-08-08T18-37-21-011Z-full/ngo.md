### about · text

**heading**

Building Futures Together

**body**

Founded in 2015 by a group of local educators, our NGO created after‑school classes to give every child a safe space to learn, explore, and grow. We envision a community where every student, regardless of background, gains the skills and confidence to succeed beyond the classroom. In the past three years, we’ve served 1,200 children, raised literacy scores by 18%, and provided over 5,000 hours of tutoring—all thanks to our supporters.
