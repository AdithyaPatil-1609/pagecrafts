3. `services` / `cards` — Pricing and detail cards for our signature portrait, editorial, and commercial photography packages.
4. `gallery` / `masonry` — A curated grid of our finest, high-contrast and moody portfolio images across various projects.

6. `contact` / `form` — A simple booking inquiry form for clients to reserve their session and discuss creative direction.
7. `footer` / `simple` — Clean, minimalist footer containing social media links, copyright, and basic studio information.
