At our humble spot in Jayanagar, every sunrise brings the aroma of freshly ground idli batter and steaming dosa pans. We opened our doors to share the comforting flavors of South Indian mornings with our diners, believing that a simple, wholesome breakfast can start the day right. Inspired by family recipes and a love for community, we keep each bite authentic, fresh, and made with care.
