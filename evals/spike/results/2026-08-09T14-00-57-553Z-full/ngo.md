### about · text

**heading**

Our Journey, Impact, and Values

**body**

Founded in 2015 by a group of educators and parents, our NGO has grown from a single classroom to a network of after‑school programs serving over 2,000 children each year. With the support of volunteers and generous supporters, we provide safe, engaging learning spaces where kids explore science, art, and teamwork. Our impact is measured in brighter smiles, improved grades, and stronger confidence as students discover their passions. Guided by the values of empowerment, inclusion, and community partnership, we strive every day to create a nurturing environment that sparks curiosity and builds a brighter future for every child.
