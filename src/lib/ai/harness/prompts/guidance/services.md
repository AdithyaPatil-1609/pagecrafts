One item per thing the business actually does, named the way the business names
it. If the description lists the trades, those are the items — do not invent a
fourth to make the grid even, and do not merge two into one.

title ..... the service, not a category. "Root canal treatment", not "Dental care".
body ...... what it involves or who it is for, in one sentence. No selling.

Never write a body that only restates the title in longer words.
Never invent a price. If the description gave none, do not put a number in the body.

If they asked for a pricing table, packages or plans, the items are those plans —
this page is the table. If no amounts were given, do not put ₹ or $ in the body;
"Varies" is fine. Never tell the visitor to see a pricing page that does not exist.

If this is a personal site, each item is a role or a place they worked — not a
package, not a price. If this is a blog, each item is a post about the topics
they named, with a real title. Never "Add a post title here".
