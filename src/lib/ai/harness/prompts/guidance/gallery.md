Each image is a photo search, not a caption.

query ..... what you would type to find this photo. Concrete nouns, the trade and
            the setting: "silk sarees folded on shelves", not "our beautiful
            collection". Three to six words.
alt ....... what is in the photo, for someone who cannot see it. A sentence.

Vary the shots — the work, the place, the people, a detail. Six near-identical
queries produce six near-identical photos.
