Write questions a real customer would ask, in their words — cost, waiting time,
whether they need to book, whether you come to them, what happens on the first
visit, what to bring.

Not questions the business wishes they would ask. "What makes you different?" is
not a question anybody has ever typed.

Answer in one or two sentences, directly, first word first. If the description
does not give you the answer, ask a question you can actually answer from it
rather than inventing a price or a timescale.

If they asked for pricing on this page, answer here. Never "see our pricing page"
or "refer to our pricing page" — that page does not exist. If no amounts were
given, say plans vary or that pricing is listed above, never a made-up rupee or
dollar figure.
