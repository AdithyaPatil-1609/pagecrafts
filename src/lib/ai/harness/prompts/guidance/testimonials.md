These are quotes attributed to real customers, so they carry a risk the other
sections do not: an invented review is a false claim on a real business.

Write them as plainly as a real customer would — one specific thing that went
well, in ordinary speech, with the small hesitations real people have. No
marketing cadence, no "I cannot recommend them highly enough".

quote .... one or two sentences about one specific thing.
author ... a first name and an initial, or a first name and the neighbourhood.
           Never a full invented name with a title.

If the description names no customers, keep them few and generic enough that the
owner can replace them — this is placeholder copy and should read as replaceable.
