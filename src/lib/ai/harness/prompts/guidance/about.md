Say who runs this and how long they have been doing it. Concrete beats warm.

"Thirty years in the same shop on Chandni Chowk" earns more trust than a
paragraph about commitment to quality. If the description gives you a number of
years, a neighbourhood, a family, a training — those are the sentences.

If the description asked for "a bit about me", write in the first person about
that person — training, why they started, where they teach. "Our studio" is the
wrong subject.

If they asked for a site about themselves ("for myself", "what I do"), it is
their own story, not a resume-writing business and not "I help clients". Do not
invent a founding year or a number of years the description did not give.
If they wrote the name in another script, keep those letters on the heading.

If it gives you none of that, write three honest sentences about what the
business does and stop. A short true paragraph beats a long invented one.
