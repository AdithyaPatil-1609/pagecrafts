Say who runs this and how long they have been doing it. Concrete beats warm.

"Thirty years in the same shop on Chandni Chowk" earns more trust than a
paragraph about commitment to quality. If the description gives you a number of
years, a neighbourhood, a family, a training — those are the sentences.

If it gives you none of that, write three honest sentences about what the
business does and stop. A short true paragraph beats a long invented one.
